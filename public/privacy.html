ShowTracker privacy policy

Effective 5 November 2026 · Developer: Ebrar Dushullovci

ShowTracker lets you track TV shows, anime and movies. This policy covers the iPhone app and website.

Data you save

For an email account, we store your email address and a protected password hash. Guest accounts use an assigned identifier. We store the profile details you choose to add, your library, watched history, favorites, lists, viewing preferences and any names you enter for watching with others. Profile images are stored as external image URLs.

How data is used

Your account data is used to sign you in, sync your progress, show your watchlist and schedule, and calculate your statistics. Technical request and error information may be processed to operate and protect the service. This version has no advertising or cross-app tracking. We do not sell your personal information.

Services and sharing

Convex provides account authentication and synced data storage. Netlify hosts the website. Catalogue information and images come from TMDB, AniList, TVMaze, Jikan and their image hosts. Catalogue requests may send search terms, title identifiers and your IP address to those services; we do not send them your account email or private watch history. Service providers may process information in other countries.

TV Time import files are read on your device. The resulting tracking data is synced to your account. If you create a sharing link, its selected catalogue entries and the heading, message and sender name you supply are accessible to anyone with that link. Your private watch history and account identifier are not included. You can revoke a link, and links expire after 30 days.

Retention and account deletion

Account data is kept while your account is active. You can request permanent deletion in Profile → Settings → Delete account, including for a guest account. After confirmation you are signed out, and your account, library, history, favorites, lists, preferences and shared links are removed. Removal from active systems normally completes within 24 hours. Limited backups and security logs may remain for the service providers’ retention periods; they are not used to reactivate your account.

Files or images you previously exported, and copies other people saved, are outside ShowTracker’s control.

Your choices and contact

You can edit your profile and tracking data in the app, revoke sharing links, or delete your account. For privacy questions or data requests, email ebrar.dushullovci@gmail.com. Please do not include your password.
