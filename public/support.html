ShowTracker support

For help with sign-in, your library, imports or the app, email ebrar.dushullovci@gmail.com.

Include your device, app version and a short description of what happened. Never send your password. You can also report a bug on GitHub; keep account details and other personal information out of public reports.

Delete your account

Open Profile → Settings → Delete account, read the confirmation and type DELETE. Deletion is permanent and normally completes within 24 hours. This also works for guest accounts.
